# FusionOps product strategy

Accepted direction, 2026-10-13. Commercial directions remain hypotheses until verified with authorized customer data and an agreed pilot. This document organizes product decisions; it does not turn every market idea into an implementation commitment. The full [architecture objective](flagship-vision.md) and [G8–G30+ programme](ultimate-roadmap.md) remain independent of any one market.

## Product boundary

FusionOps Core owns reusable C# capabilities: versioned commitments, independently validated planning, exact-plan approval, authoritative reservation, durable effects/recovery, actual facts and reconciliation. Product modules translate a chosen industry's data and user decisions into those contracts. They cannot bypass tenant/project authority, substitute a simulated capacity for ownership, reinterpret historical approval or issue untracked external effects.

The initial industrial-installation hypothesis remains described in [commercial-pilot.md](commercial-pilot.md). Vehicle-carrier logistics is a second research direction in [logistics-feasibility.md](logistics-feasibility.md). Neither has verified customer demand or production integrations. A Planning Studio, API license, SaaS, private deployment and integration services are distribution options to compare against customer evidence, not five products to build simultaneously.

## Focus and responsibilities

Until G7 is complete, use the proposed **85% Core / 15% logistics research** as a planning heuristic, not a time allocation claim or scheduler. Core retains implementation priority. Research can define data requirements, compare models and prepare experiments without forcing transport-specific concepts into the universal domain.

The proposed responsibility split is: Artem validates prospective users/buyers, current process, pricing and product scenarios; the owner leads architecture, algorithms, engineering quality and technical feasibility. This is a proposed collaboration model, not evidence that Artem accepted tasks. No outreach, messaging or assignment is performed by this document. The two streams meet at an agreed client problem and a versioned technical contract.

## Evidence before a product commitment

1. Identify a recurring costly operational decision and its actual owner. Record current tools, a recent incident, available authority and data gaps.
2. Obtain permitted, anonymized data with source revisions and definitions of time/cost/constraints. Distinguish source observations, proposals, reservations and facts.
3. Freeze a comparison dataset and customer baseline. Validate both baseline and proposed decisions under comparable constraints; report unsupported requirements instead of silently relaxing them.
4. Measure planning/review effort, feasibility, delays, modeled cost, practical change burden and adopted outcomes. Include import/integration/support costs; separate opportunity from realized money.
5. Agree a bounded pilot and pass/fail criteria before tuning against the data. Customer acceptance and willingness to pay are separate from solver quality.
6. Promote a repeatable product need into an owning module/ADR/engineering gate. If the experiment fails, record why; Core development continues.
